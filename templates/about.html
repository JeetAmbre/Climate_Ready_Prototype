About ClimateReady

ClimateReady was developed as a prototype decision-support platform for understanding local climate vulnerability and connecting spatial risk assessment with practical local action.

The Idea

Climate risks are experienced locally, but climate information is often presented at scales or formats that are difficult to translate into local decisions.

Our Approach

ClimateReady aims to bridge this gap by combining spatial risk information with an accessible interface and location-specific recommendations.
