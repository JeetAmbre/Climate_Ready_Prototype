From Climate Risk to Local Action

ClimateReady is a geospatial climate-resilience planning and decision-support platform helping turn localised Urban Heat and Waterlogging risk data into actionable adaptation plans without requiring GIS expertise.

Start Assessment → Learn Methodology

Stage 1: Risk Profile

Interactive satellite risk assessment mapping localized Urban Heat and Waterlogging vulnerabilities.

Stage 2: Intervention Discovery

Catalog-backed scientific interventions matched specifically to underlying drivers and local settings.

Stage 3: Decision Planner

Evidence-based project reporting, INR costing benchmarks, and climate ROI evaluation for funders.

Stage 4: Monitoring & Tracking

Longitudinal satellite verification framework to track post-implementation cooling and runoff mitigation.

Two Critical Dimensions of Urban Climate Risk

Understand where elevated urban surface heat and heat stress vulnerability are concentrated, exploring surface temperature, built-up intensity, and vegetation deficits.

The 4-Stage Adaptation Planning Workflow

A systematic evidence-based journey from multi-hazard satellite observation to on-ground resilience implementation.

  1. 1

    Risk Profile

    Multi-hazard geospatial assessment across district and subdistrict administrative units.

  2. 2

    Intervention Discovery

    Contextual filtering of CPWD/NDMA-benchmarked nature-based and structural solutions.

  3. 3

    Decision Planner

    Comprehensive cost in INR, cooling/runoff impact metrics, and downloadable project dossiers.

  4. 4

    Monitoring & Tracking

    Satellite observation framework ready for tracking baseline changes and resilience gains.
