About ClimateReady

Empowering cities and grassroots stakeholders to transform satellite climate data into targeted, locally budgeted urban resilience interventions without requiring GIS expertise.

The Problem We Target

The Urban Climate Vulnerability Crisis in India

Indian cities face escalating climate hazards characterized by intense Urban Heat Island (+3–4°C UHI effect) and recurrent monsoon waterlogging. Rapid densification, loss of green canopies, and expanding impervious concrete surfaces magnify thermal stress and stormwater accumulation in vulnerable neighborhoods.

Despite these mounting risks, local municipal authorities, NGOs, and CSR teams encounter critical roadblocks:

1. Inaccessible Localized Spatial Data

Macro-level climate projections lack ward-level granularity, leaving decision-makers blind to exact hyperlocal vulnerability hotspots.

2. Technical & GIS Planning Capacity Gaps

Operating complex GIS tools and satellite processing engines requires specialized expertise that grassroots and municipal departments rarely possess.

3. Absent Decision-Support & Impact Frameworks

Budget allocators struggle to match identified risks to costed, context-appropriate engineering or nature-based solutions with measurable ROI.

Our Solution

ClimateReady: An Integrated Geospatial Decision Engine

Developed by Team BhoomiVed, ClimateReady integrates satellite Earth observation with an actionable 4-stage adaptation planning workflow.

STAGE 1

Risk Profile

High-resolution Earth Engine analysis across 10+ biophysical & socio-economic indicators mapping Heat and Flood hazards at district and subdistrict levels.

STAGE 2

Intervention Discovery

Automated matching to our verified catalog of 16+ nature-based and engineering solutions (cool roofs, bioswales, Miyawaki forests, sponge parks) with CPWD cost benchmarks.

STAGE 3

Decision Planner

Quantified adaptation planning dossiers calculating INR budget estimates, surface cooling (°C), and runoff reduction potential for project approval.

STAGE 4

Monitoring & Tracking

Satellite-based verification framework designed to track post-implementation microclimate gains, NDVI canopy expansion, and surface temperature declines.

Target Users

Who ClimateReady Empowers

Local Authorities & Municipal Planners

  • Identify highest-priority wards for pre-monsoon desilting and stormwater detention infrastructure.
  • Incorporate scientific cool roof policies and green corridors into statutory city master plans.
  • Allocate municipal capital expenditure backed by peer-reviewed biophysical datasets.

NGOs & Development Organisations

  • Pinpoint vulnerable informal settlements facing acute indoor heat stress or chronic ponding.
  • Deploy community-scale modular solutions such as cool roof paints, rain gardens, and shading tensile structures.
  • Generate professional, evidence-backed funding proposals and policy briefs in minutes.

CSR Teams & Climate Funders

  • Direct CSR environmental and climate adaptation budgets towards high-impact urban micro-geographies.
  • Evaluate project feasibility with standardized CPWD unit costing (INR/sqm) and expected impact metrics.
  • Track ongoing ESG and climate resilience outcomes with defensible satellite benchmarks.
